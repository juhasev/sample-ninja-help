### Registration question options

These are the options of a radio or checkbox question. To see them, click on the arrow-down indicator on the left of the question.

**D: disqualifying option (radio and checkbox)**

Click the **D** icon on an option to make it disqualifying. A panelist who picks a disqualifying option still answers the remaining questions. At the end, the registration is submitted without the password and reCaptcha steps, and the panelist is shown the **Registration disqualified** landing page. No panelist is created.

Disqualifying options are saved for this registration survey only.

**E: exclusive option (checkbox only)**

Click the **E** icon on an option to make it exclusive. When a panelist ticks an exclusive option, all other options are unticked. When they tick another option, the exclusive option is unticked.

> **WARNING** Exclusive is a setting of the data variable option itself. It applies everywhere that data variable is used, not only in this registration survey. You can also change it when you edit the data variable in **Data Variables**.
