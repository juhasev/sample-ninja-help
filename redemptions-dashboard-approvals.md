## Redemption Approvals

These charts show how your panelists' redemption requests were handled over the selected period (the last 3, 6, 9 or 12 months). There is one chart per reward currency and sub panel, so you can see at a glance how much is being approved, how much is still waiting and how much was rejected.

### What the charts show

Each column is one day, or one week when the period is longer than 3 months. A week starts on Monday. The column is split by the redemption's current status:

- **Approved** (green) — redemptions that were approved and sent to the reward partner.
- **Pending** (grey) — redemptions still waiting for someone to approve or reject them.
- **Rejected** (red) — redemptions that were rejected. The points were refunded to the panelist.

By default the columns show the **value** of the rewards in the reward's own currency, for example USD or GBP. Turn on **Frequencies** to show the **number** of redemptions instead.

> Redemptions are shown in the day or week they were **requested**, not the day they were approved or rejected. A redemption requested last week and approved today moves from grey to green in last week's column.

> Days and weeks are in **UTC**, so a redemption made late in the evening in the Americas can appear on the next day.

### Download

The download button exports the numbers behind all the charts as one CSV file, with one row per day or week, currency and sub panel. Each row has the approved, pending and rejected counts and values. The rows match the charts: daily for 3 months, weekly for longer periods.

Redemptions that are not linked to a sub panel have an empty sub panel cell. The sub panel column is left out when none of your redemptions are linked to a sub panel.

### How to use it

- **Keep the queue moving.** A growing grey section means redemption requests are waiting. Panelists who wait a long time for their rewards lose trust in the panel. Approve or reject them in the **APPROVALS** tab.
- **Spot fraud.** A sudden rise in rejections, or a burst of redemptions much larger than usual, can mean fraudulent accounts are trying to cash out. Review the panelists' **Quality Score** before approving.
- **Plan your budget.** The approved value per currency shows how much you are paying out to reward partners, and the pending value shows what is about to be paid.
- **Compare sub panels.** If one sub panel has far more rejections than the others, check its recruitment sources.

> Approved redemptions that later failed at the reward partner are still shown as approved, even though the points were refunded to the panelist. Check the **HISTORY** tab for partner statuses.
